 Opening generally smoothes the contour of an
object, breaks narrow isthmuses, and eliminates thin protrusions. Closing also tends to smooth sections of contours, but, as opposed to opening, it generally fuses narrow
breaks and long thin gulfs, eliminates small holes, and fills gaps in the contour.
Thus, the opening A by B is the erosion of A by B, followed by a dilation of the result
by B.
